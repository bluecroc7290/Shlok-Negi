Off Grid

There is something comforting about stepping away from the usual process for a while. No framework, no checklist, no reference to compare yourself against.

I think creativity needs some of that space. When everything you make is measured against what already exists, it becomes very easy to forget why you started making things in the first place.

Sometimes the best way to find a better direction is simply to stop looking at everyone else's.

Stop following the map

There is usually a recommended way of doing things. Learn this tool, follow this process, build this portfolio, post this often.

Some of it is useful. But following every rule can make your work technically correct and completely forgettable.

Make room for weird ideas

The ideas that don't immediately make sense are sometimes the ones worth exploring.

Not every experiment needs to become a finished product. Sometimes making something just because it feels interesting is enough. It keeps the work personal.
