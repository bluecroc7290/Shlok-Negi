Signal

The internet has made it incredibly easy to make noise. Every day there are new products, new ideas, new opinions and new things asking for our attention.

Somewhere in all of that noise, I think there is still a lot of value in being able to recognize a signal. Something that actually deserves your attention instead of simply demanding it.

This applies to products too. The best ones usually don't need to shout. They solve a real problem clearly enough that you notice the difference.

Attention is expensive

We tend to think of attention as something we have an unlimited supply of. It isn't. Every notification, tab and piece of content takes a small part of it.

Good design respects that. It doesn't make every element equally important. It tells you where to look and lets everything else stay quiet.

Make the signal obvious

Whether it's a landing page or a simple interface, clarity usually comes from deciding what matters most.

Once that decision is made, the rest becomes easier. You can remove distractions, simplify the interface and let the important thing speak for itself.
