Side Effects

Every decision has consequences that aren't always part of the original plan. Sometimes they're useful. Sometimes they're the exact reason something stops working.

Design is full of these little side effects. A button becomes larger to make it easier to notice, and suddenly the entire interface feels heavier. An animation makes something feel alive, but now the page feels slower.

None of these decisions are necessarily wrong. They just remind me that every choice changes something else.

Nothing exists alone

I've started thinking about interfaces less as collections of individual components and more as systems where everything affects everything around it.

Change the spacing and the hierarchy changes. Change the typeface and the personality changes. Change one interaction and the whole rhythm can feel different.

The small things matter

The details are usually where the character of a product starts to appear.

A thoughtful transition, a strange little interaction or even an unusually good empty state can make something feel considered. Those things aren't always necessary, but they're often what people remember.
