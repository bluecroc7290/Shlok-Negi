Field Notes

I've always found it interesting how much of design happens before you actually start designing.

The obvious part is opening Figma, creating a frame, choosing a typeface and moving things around until they look right. But the decisions that actually shape the work usually happen much earlier.

You decide what matters, what doesn't, what deserves attention and what can quietly disappear into the background.

Start with less

Good interfaces rarely need to explain themselves loudly. The hierarchy should do most of the talking.

This is something I've been trying to understand better through my own work. Removing something is often more valuable than adding another component, animation or decorative element.

Design is mostly editing

The first version is rarely the good version. The interesting work usually starts when you begin removing things that seemed important five minutes ago.

Maybe that's what I enjoy most about design. It's less about making something impressive and more about figuring out what deserves to remain.
